# Code of Conduct

RapidRepo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews, is expected to follow it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work rather than the person. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@tibor-horvath](https://github.com/tibor-horvath), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is inappropriate.
